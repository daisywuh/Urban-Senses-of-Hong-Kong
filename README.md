# Urban Senses of Hong Kong

An interactive map-based digital archive that explores Hong Kong through Cantopop, screen memories, local food, public transport and community-contributed stories.

[Explore the Live Project](https://daisywuh.github.io/Urban-Senses-of-Hong-Kong/)

## Project Overview

Urban Senses of Hong Kong investigates how music, moving images, food and everyday journeys can shape our memories of a city.

Instead of presenting Hong Kong as a conventional geographic map, the project treats the city as an emotional and cultural archive. Visitors can navigate between different locations, discover their connections with Cantopop songs and screen culture, follow animated trams and ferries, explore local food, and contribute personal memories to the map.

The project combines cultural storytelling with interaction design and front-end development, transforming fragmented urban memories into an explorable digital experience.

## Key Features

* Interactive map-based exploration of Hong Kong
* Location-based Cantopop and screen memories
* Animated Hong Kong trams and Star Ferry routes
* Illustrated icons representing local food and landmarks
* Historical and present-day visual comparisons
* Filterable categories of urban memories
* User-contributed stories and photographs
* Responsive interface for desktop and mobile devices

## Design Concept

The visual language draws inspiration from Hong Kong’s coastline, mountainous landscape, public transport and everyday street culture.

A soft colour palette and illustrated interface elements create an approachable atmosphere, while the map provides a spatial structure for connecting different forms of media. The experience is designed to feel less like searching a database and more like wandering through collective memories of the city.

## Interaction

Users can:

1. Explore memory points across the map.
2. Select different cultural categories.
3. Open location cards containing songs, images and stories.
4. Follow animated tram and ferry journeys.
5. compare historical scenes with their present-day locations.
6. Add their own memories and photographs.
7. Track how many locations they have illuminated.
